About Us

SubLink is a platform designed to tackle the most difficult part of the subletting process: the search. We understand the struggle of utilizing multiple platforms to find a leaser/leasee, and now all those platforms can be replaced with SubLink. We provide compatability scores for living spaces and potential roommates, so your future home will be just right.
