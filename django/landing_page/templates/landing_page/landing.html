About Us

SubLink is a platform designed to tackle the most difficult part of the subletting process: the search. We understand the struggle of utilizing multiple platforms to find a leaser/leasee, and now all those platforms can be replaced with SubLink. We provide compatability scores for living spaces and potential roommates, so your future home will be just right.

Searching... Down to a Science

With our custom algorithm, we curate compatability scores for potential living spaces and roommates, tailored to you.

Made By and For BC Students

Although we have plans for expansion, SubLink is currently only for Boston College students with verified emails.

Secure, Simple, SubLink

With an easy sign-up process with Google OAuth and clean user experience, our platform is perfect for you.
